MAPLESTORY IDLE OPTIMIZER 2.6.9 — COMPANION ROLE MENU FIX
==========================================================

PROBLEM
On affected Linux/Tk window managers, clicking a companion's role/equip badge
posted the native Tk menu during the original mouse-press callback. The release
from that same click could then dismiss the menu immediately, making it appear
for only a split second.

FIX
- Role-menu posting is delayed briefly until the initiating click is complete.
- A pending popup is cancelled before another is scheduled.
- The menu uses dark text on its light background for consistent readability.
- A regression test verifies delayed scheduling and rapid-click cancellation.
- The frozen startup smoke test performs a real open/select/cleanup role-menu cycle.

Optimizer calculations and saved account/build formats are unchanged.
