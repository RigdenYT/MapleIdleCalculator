MAPLESTORY IDLE OPTIMIZER 2.6.10 — PERSISTENT ROLE CHOOSER FIX
================================================================

PROBLEM
The delayed native Tk menu still behaved as a press-and-hold menu on the affected
Linux/Tk environment. Releasing the badge click closed it, and dragging to an
option did not activate a selection.

FIX
- Replaced tk.Menu entirely with an in-window Frame containing ordinary buttons.
- Opening and selecting are now two independent clicks.
- The chooser stays visible until selection, Cancel, Escape, a second badge open,
  or an outside click.
- The packaged GUI smoke test confirms the chooser remains open after the badge release, then invokes the Main button through Tk's normal command path. This avoids unreliable synthetic pointer warping in frozen builds.

Optimizer calculations and saved account/build formats are unchanged.
